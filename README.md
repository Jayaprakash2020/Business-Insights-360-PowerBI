# 📊 Business Insights 360 – Power BI Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview

Business Insights 360 is an interactive Power BI dashboard designed
to provide a comprehensive view of business performance across
Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive functions.

The dashboard transforms business data into actionable insights
through interactive KPIs, benchmark comparisons, trend analysis,
and detailed product, customer, market, and regional analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to help business stakeholders
monitor key performance indicators, identify performance gaps,
understand business trends, and make data-driven decisions.

The dashboard provides different analytical perspectives for
different business functions while maintaining a unified reporting
experience.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

The dashboard enables analysis of:

- Sales performance against benchmarks and previous-year results.
- Gross margin and net profit performance across business areas.
- Top-performing customers and products based on revenue contribution.
- Market and regional differences in profitability.
- Forecast accuracy and supply-chain risk.
- Revenue contribution across different business dimensions.
- Trends in key business KPIs over time.

---

## 📊 Key Features

### Interactive Analysis

Users can dynamically analyze the business using filters such as:

- Region / Country
- Store
- Category
- Fiscal Year
- Quarter
- YTD / YTG
- Benchmark

### Benchmark Analysis

Performance can be compared against:

- Last Year (LY)
- Business Benchmark (BM)
- Target

### Dynamic Analysis

The dashboard allows users to switch analytical dimensions
and explore different levels of business performance.
