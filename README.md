# Business-Insights-360-PowerBI
Interactive Power BI dashboard providing 360° business insights across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive functions.
__

##  Project Overview

- This project analyzes the **business performance of AtliQ Hardware** using Microsoft Power BI.
- It provides a **360° view of business performance** across Finance, Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Executive functions.
- The dashboard converts business data into interactive visualizations and actionable insights to support data-driven decision-making.


##  Project Objectives

### Finance Analysis
- Analyze Net Sales, Gross Margin, and Net Profit
- Compare financial performance against benchmarks
- Analyze Profit & Loss performance across different time periods and markets

### Sales Analysis
- Analyze customer and product performance
- Compare Net Sales and Gross Margin
- Identify top-performing customers and products
- Analyze sales trends and growth

### Marketing Analysis
- Analyze product and market performance
- Compare profitability across different markets and regions
- Identify high and low-performing segments

### Supply Chain Analysis
- Analyze Forecast Accuracy
- Monitor Net Error and Absolute Error
- Identify products, customers, and segments with supply chain risks

### Executive Analysis
- Provide a high-level overview of key business KPIs
- Consolidate important insights from different business functions
- Support management-level decision-making
